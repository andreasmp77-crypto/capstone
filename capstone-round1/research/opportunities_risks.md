# Opportunities & Risks

## Opportunities

* **Earlier risk identification**
  A decision-support tool could help crewing teams identify crew-change plans with multiple warning signs—such as tight travel connections, document issues or vessel schedule uncertainty—before they become last-minute problems.

* **Reduced manual workload**
  Crewing information is often distributed across emails, spreadsheets, travel itineraries and operational systems. A central risk briefing could help officers prioritise which cases require attention first.

* **Better operational visibility**
  Combining available operational data with clear risk indicators could provide managers with a clearer overview of upcoming crew-change exposure and recurring disruption patterns.

## Risks

* **Data quality and availability**
  Crew-change information may be incomplete, outdated or distributed across multiple systems. Public data may also provide only indirect indicators rather than real-time operational information.

* **Over-reliance on AI**
  Users could incorrectly treat an AI-generated risk briefing as a definitive decision or prediction rather than decision support.

* **Privacy and compliance**
  Crew information may include personal, employment and travel-related data that requires appropriate access controls and responsible handling.

## Mitigation Strategies

* Use transparent rules and clearly distinguish between verified data, risk indicators and assumptions.
* Keep a human crewing officer responsible for reviewing the briefing and making operational decisions.
* Limit the Round 1 POC to non-sensitive or synthetic data and avoid autonomous decisions.
* Maintain clear explanations showing why a crew-change plan was flagged as higher risk.
* **Implement logging and monitoring** to record system inputs, outputs and identified risk factors, helping to detect errors, inconsistencies and unexpected system behaviour.
* **Provide a dynamic, connected dashboard** to give stakeholders clear visibility of crew-change risks, operational trends and system outputs, supporting monitoring and informed human decision-making.
* **Design a clear, useful and intuitive UI/UX** that makes risk information easy to understand and actions easy to identify, reducing the likelihood of user errors, misunderstandings or important warnings being overlooked.
